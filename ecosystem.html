Solana Ecosystem

From protocol primitives to complete workflows.

Composable modules for assets, permissions, payments, governance and financial protocols, extended into games, content, devices and research.

Rust / Anchor / TypeScript SDK / React / Groth16

Explore the full scope ↓Discuss this project ↗
01 / The problem

Each domain has distinct permissions, precision rules and state machines. The platform shares interfaces and transaction feedback without flattening those differences.

02 / My role

Two-person collaboration. My A-side scope covers assets, distribution, permissions, payments, governance, proof consumption, GameFi, digital rights, content and research, plus shared interfaces, frontend integration and reproducibility. B-side financial modules are credited as collaborative work.

Full implementation scope

M01 · Digital assets+

SPL, selected Token-2022 extensions, NFTs, membership credentials and compressed assets.

M02 · Distribution+

Allowlists, airdrops, vesting and claims with replay and balance controls.

M03 · Identity+

Wallet sessions, eligibility, delegated permissions, limits and revocation.

M04 · Payments+

Payment requests, batch and recurring transfers, and Project 1 escrow integration.

M05 · Governance+

Organization approvals, multisig, voting, delayed execution and permission changes.

M06 · Trading+

Collaborative module: AMM, liquidity, limit orders and escrowed NFT sales.

M07 · Lending+

Collaborative module: collateral, time-based interest, shares, repayment and liquidation.

M08 · Staking+

Collaborative module: native staking, reward accounting, cooldown and exit.

M09 · Margin+

Collaborative module: margin, P&L, insurance funds and liquidation boundaries.

M10 · Oracles+

Collaborative module: price validity, circuit breakers and local ORAO randomness integration.

M11 · Private eligibility+

Consumes real Project 3 Groth16 proofs, with atomic nullifier checks and reward claims.

Scenarios & perspectives

S01 · GameFi+

Item claims, consumption, rewards, trading and randomness.

S02 · DePIN+

Signed reports, replay protection and authorized rewards; software-simulated device boundaries are explicit.

S03 · Digital rights+

Registration, evidence, permitted transfers and redemption states; not proof of physical asset delivery.

S04 · Creator content+

Private content, membership or paid access, revenue splits and refunds.

S05 · Bounded AI agents+

Budgets, payee allowlists, execution limits, revocation and audit commitments.

S06 · Research collaboration+

Research records, funding tasks, private delivery and escrow-based review.
